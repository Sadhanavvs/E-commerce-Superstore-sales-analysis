# E-commerce-Superstore-sales-analysis
 ## Project Overview
 This project analyses retail sales data to understand revenue trends, product performance,and regional sales distribution. The analysis helps identify key business insights that can support data-driven decision making.
 # Obejective
 * Analyse overall sales and profit performance
 * Identify top-performing product categories
 * Compare sales across regions
 * Understand the relationship between discounts and profit
 * Visaualize sales trends overtime
 # Tools Used
 Power BI
 Pandas
 # Key Insights
 * Technology category genrates the highest sales revenue
 * The west region contributes the largest share of overall sales.
 * A small group of products accounts for a large protion of revenue.
 * Higher discounts often reduce overall profitability.
